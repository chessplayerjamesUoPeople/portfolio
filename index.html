M.S. IT Capstone · 2026 · In progress

Amigo Alert

Many older adults live alone, and after a fall or stroke they may not be able to call for help for hours. Most monitoring products rely on wearables that are expensive and come off in the shower. Amigo Alert uses the phone people already own instead: it sends a check-in twice a day and, if two prompts go unanswered or the emergency button is pressed, texts a consented emergency contact.

I built it as a .NET MAUI Android app backed by an ASP.NET API, a minute-by-minute Azure WebJob, a Blazor consent page, and Azure SQL. Escalation runs on the server, so a dead phone raises an alert instead of hiding one. In testing it escalated 20 of 20 missed check-ins and queued SMS alerts in 0.40 seconds on average.

Engineering thesis · Universidad Técnica del Norte · 2022

Cóndor Compiler

Programming courses at my university had high failure and dropout rates, and nearly every language students learn uses English keywords. For my engineering thesis I created Cóndor, a beginner language with Spanish keywords and built-in sound and graphics, along with a beginner-friendly editor that has live error checking and code templates.

I wrote the single-pass compiler by hand in C#: a table-driven lexer, an LALR(1) parser that recovers from simple mistakes, and a semantic analyzer that generates C# and runs it. Across 3,854 logged student compilations and a 40-person survey, 92.5% said they would recommend it to someone learning to program.
